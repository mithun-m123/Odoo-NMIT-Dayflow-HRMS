Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for leave?
Open the Leave section, select leave type and dates, then submit your request.
How is my work time calculated?
Your productive work time starts after Check In. Break duration is excluded from the total worked time.
Can I update my personal details?
Yes. Open My Profile, edit your details and click Save Changes.
